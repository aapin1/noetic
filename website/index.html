A memory for everything you watchthinkread

Mneme reads what you save, connects it to what you already believe, and draws the map as it grows.

Before the nine muses there were three: Melete for practice, Aoide for song, and Mneme for memory.

This one is for Mneme.

the map

Atlas

Everything you save lands as a point on a dark map, near the ideas it already agrees with. Topics form on their own and drift as you change. Zoom into one and it opens as a smaller map of its own.

the movement

Mind

Atlas shows where your knowledge lives. Mind reports what is moving through it: a thread picking up speed, or two saves that quietly disagree. When something deserves your attention, it says so.

the record

You

The long view of your own attention. A timeline of everything you have saved, streaks and dry spells included. Come back in a month and see what changed.

the pulse

Social

A slow pulse from the few people you follow. What your friends have been saving lately, and which topics they are deep in. It is a check-in, not a feed.
